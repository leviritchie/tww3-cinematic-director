[h1]Cinematic Director[/h1]

Watch your battles like a film, and let your regiments fight them by the plans you gave them in the campaign.

[b]Not tested in multiplayer.[/b] See the notes at the bottom.

[h2]Unit tactics[/h2]
In the campaign, select an army and click [b]Battle Tactics[/b] above the unit cards. Give each regiment a short list of rows, checked from the top. Each row is [i]When[/i] (a condition) > [i]Do[/i] (an action) > [i]Target[/i]. For example: "Enemy missiles within 200 m > Cycle charge > Enemy from WHEN", or "Always > Guard > Nearest ally".
[list]
[*][b]Plays[/b] are actions with several steps: Hammer and anvil (wait until your line is fighting, then charge the enemy from behind), flank charge, wide sweep, cycle charge, skirmish, focus fire, fire support, hunt missiles, kill the general, take the high ground, duel, battle mage
[*]More rows unlock as a unit gains rank; lords and heroes always get five
[*]Lords and heroes can cast their own spells by rule
[*]Presets for common roles: hold the line, hold the hill, flanking infantry, vanguard, bodyguard, missile screen, and more
[*][b]Paradigms[/b]: up to four sets of tactics per unit, unlocked by your general's rank (1, 5, 12, 20). Switch the whole army between them in battle with the paradigm buttons
[*]During deployment you can still change any unit's tactics in the battle tactics panel (for that battle only)
[/list]
Once the battle starts, your regiments carry out their tactics on their own and [b]you can't give orders[/b]. Units with no tactics set (custom battles, reinforcements) use defaults for their role.

[h2]The cinematic camera[/h2]
The director takes the camera when deployment ends. Press the [b]Toggle camera tracking[/b] or [b]Unit camera[/b] key (Options > Controls) to take it back or hand it over again.
[list]
[*]It cuts to the most interesting fighting: charges, clashes, volleys, spells, monsters and heroes, units breaking
[*]Close charge cam: rides in beside your cavalry, chariots or monsters as they charge
[*]Brace cam: watches from behind your line as enemy cavalry hits it
[*]Close-ups of heavy hits from spells and artillery
[*]Short slow motion at the moment of impact (single-player only)
[*]Callouts over the units say what is happening, in green when it's good for you and red when it's bad
[*]Smooth glides between shots, filmed from your side of the field
[*]When one side breaks, it films the rout and the victors
[/list]
The unit cards stay at the bottom of the screen while filming. Put the mouse over a card (or click it) to see that unit's tactics. The [b]HUD[/b] button in the top right cycles letterbox > no HUD > full HUD (with a tide-of-battle bar).

[h2]Notes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Multiplayer: not tested.[/b] Unit tactics switch themselves off in multiplayer battles, so everyone keeps control of their own army, and slow motion is off. The tactics editor has no multiplayer safeguard yet, so don't edit tactics in a multiplayer campaign.
[*]Safe to add to an existing campaign.
[*]Script-only mod with its own UI templates. It doesn't replace any game files, so it should work with most other mods.
[*]Problems? The game folder has [i]cinematic_director_log.txt[/i]. Please include it in your report.
[*]Source code (MIT): [url=https://github.com/leviritchie/tww3-cinematic-director]github.com/leviritchie/tww3-cinematic-director[/url]
[/list]
